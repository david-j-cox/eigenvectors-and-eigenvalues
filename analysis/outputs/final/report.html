Why switch rate is dropped

Noise ratio per subject. Green passes the 0.6 threshold, red fails.

Usable for five subjects of thirteen. The pooled figure the diagnostics report, 0.419, passes — but pooling is the wrong operation for a design whose unit is the individual. Reward rate, by contrast, passes for all thirteen (0.311–0.537).
